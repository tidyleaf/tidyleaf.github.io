# Privacy Policy - Tidyleaf Form Filler - No Daily Limit

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Publisher: Tidyleaf

## Summary

This extension does not collect, sell or share personal data. We run no server and have no analytics.

## What stays on your device

Your profiles (the names, addresses and other values you type into Options), your site rules (values read from forms when you press "Save this form"), the selected profile and your optional license key are saved with `chrome.storage.local`. Chrome keeps them in your browser profile on this device. They are not synced and we cannot see them. Removing the extension deletes them.

## What the extension reads

Only when you click the toolbar button, press "Fill this page" or "Save this form", or use the keyboard shortcut, the extension runs on the page you are looking at to find its form fields and fill them, or to read the values you typed so you can save them as a rule. It does not run on any page otherwise. It never reads, saves or fills password fields, card numbers, card security codes or one-time codes.

## Network requests

Filling and saving make no network request. The only request the extension ever makes is the Pro license check: if you enter a license key, the key is sent to `api.polar.sh` (Polar, the payment provider that sold you the key) to confirm it is valid, about once a day. Nothing else is sent: no form values, page addresses or identifiers. Polar receives your IP address as any web request does and handles it under its own privacy policy. Without a license key the extension never contacts anyone.

## What we do not do

- No collection of browsing history, page contents or form values.
- No advertising, tracking, fingerprinting or analytics.
- No remote code: everything the extension runs ships inside the package.
- No sale or transfer of data to third parties.

Changes will be posted at this address with a new date. Questions: contact the publisher through the support link on the Chrome Web Store listing.
